Two card themes — Visa & Mastercard

Built from the uploaded product render and the live app tokens. The Visa theme reproduces the black-metallic card with the lime neon edge and orbital field. The Mastercard theme keeps the same brand mark and layout but shifts to a graphite→emerald body so the two are instantly distinguishable in a wallet. Both are virtual cards (no embossing), ISO ID-1 proportions.
