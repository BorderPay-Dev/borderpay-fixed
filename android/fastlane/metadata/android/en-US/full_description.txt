BorderPay helps businesses manage global payments from one secure mobile app.

Open supported business receiving accounts, manage Digital Dollar wallets, receive funds, send payments, and track activity from a clean mobile dashboard.

Key features:
• Direct BorderPay Business accounts
• Secure business verification
• Supported USD, EUR and GBP receiving accounts
• USDC, USDT and EURC wallet access where eligible
• Global and African payment rails in supported markets
• Send and receive payment flows
• Transaction activity and notifications
• Business team access and controls
• Developer API and white-label access for approved partners
• PIN, biometric and multi-factor security

Direct BorderPay signup is for businesses. Approved API and white-label partners may provide authorized end-user experiences under their own onboarding policies.

Availability of accounts, wallets, currencies and payment corridors depends on verification status, country, compliance review and supported provider rails.

BorderPay Africa is built for compliant global business payments, with Africa as its primary market.
